The [graph page](/graph) shows every note and project as a node, and every `[[wikilink]]` as an edge between them.

## How it works

When you write `[[Some Note]]` in any MDX file, the garden automatically:

1. Resolves the link to the correct page
2. Records it as a backlink on the target note
3. Adds an edge to the graph

At the bottom of each page you'll see **"Linked from"** — a list of everything that points back to the current note.

## Why this matters

The graph makes invisible structure visible. You can see:

- Which notes are central (many connections)
- Which notes are isolated (no connections yet)
- Unexpected clusters — ideas that are more related than you thought

It also keeps you honest. If a note has no connections, it might mean it hasn't been integrated into your thinking yet.

## Navigating the graph

- **Hover** a node to see its label
- **Click** a node to navigate to that page
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out
- **Drag** to pan around

Notes are shown in purple, projects in blue.
