## Priority 1: outline contrast in dark mode

Every sprite hardcodes a near-black outline tuned for light paper: `#20301f` on creatures, `#3a4550` and `#3a2f1a` on items. In dark mode the tile background is `--paper-raised` (`#1b1b1f`), which sits at nearly the same luminance, so silhouette edges melt into the background and the crisp pixel-art edge is lost. It affects all 11 sprites at once.

**Fix:** make the outline theme-aware. SVG `fill` accepts `var(--token)`, and the `SpriteData` palette is a plain string array, so a palette entry can hold a CSS variable reference instead of a hex literal.

1. Add sprite outline token(s) to `globals.css` under both `:root` and `.dark`. Pick either a single neutral `--sprite-outline`, or a small set if you judge that collapsing the current warm and cool outline hues into one flattens the art. Justify whichever you choose.
2. Point each sprite's outline palette index at the token.
3. **Update `validate.ts`** so a `var(--...)` palette entry is valid. Right now it may assume hex, and the validator must keep failing loudly on genuinely malformed data.
4. Verify contrast against **both** `--paper` and `--paper-raised` in both themes. The item drawer sits on `--paper-raised`, the dev page may not.

## Priority 2: `bracken` silhouette regresses

T1's finding, verified on screen: `mossling`'s canopy is four overlapping filled ellipses at near-100% internal fill, while `bracken`'s fronds are 2px `fillRect` bands separated by 1px gaps. Side by side, **stage 3 reads as thinner and less grown than stage 2**, so the line goes small, full, thinner, huge. That breaks the continuous-growth intent stated in the sprite file's own header.

Note what is *not* wrong: the 1px gaps render as clean seams at scale 2, they do not muddy. The problem is mass, not resolution.

**Fix:** give `bracken` more filled area and a more fern-like read. Thicker or overlapping frond bands, and varied frond length or angle rather than uniform stacked rectangles. The requirement is objective: **`bracken` must occupy visibly more filled pixels than `mossling`**, and the four stages must increase monotonically in mass.

Count the filled (non-transparent) pixels per stage and put the four numbers in your report as evidence.

## Priority 3: idle animation displacement

The two-frame flip is implemented correctly, but `bob` and `sway` are only ever 0 or 1, so the motion is a single raw pixel and is easy to miss at scale 1 and 2. Increase it to read as alive at scale 2 and above, without turning into a jitter. This is a judgment call, so use your eyes and say what you picked.
