## What each renders

**SpecimenPlate.** The mounted-object moment. Sprite at `scale`, creature name, `stage.index` of 4, `stage.blurb`, and an `XpBar`. This is the **one place in the entire project where a card with elevation is allowed** (DESIGN.md 2.3), because here elevation communicates a real thing: a specimen mounted on a plate. Frame it with a 1px `--rule` border, no radius.

**XpBar.** Progress through the current stage. Numbers in Geist Mono (`.font-data`). Show `xpIntoStage` over `xpForNextStage`. At max stage (`xpForNextStage === null`) show a completed state, not `NaN` and not a division by zero.

**XpLedger.** One row per `XpEntry`: label, `count`, `rate`, `xp`. Then the total. **Do not put a hairline under every row.** DESIGN.md 6 and the taste skill both ban that pattern. Group them or use sparse dividers. Entries with `count === 0` should be hidden, not shown as zeros.

**ItemDrawer.** All seven items. Unlocked ones show their sprite at full opacity with the name. Locked ones are dimmed, showing `def.requirement` and `progress` as a percentage. Locked items are the engagement hook, so the requirement text must be legible, not a mystery.

**Home page.** Put the plate near the top, above the existing content feed. Keep the existing notes and projects sections working.

## Hard rules

- **No game UI on note or project pages.** DESIGN.md section 1. That separation is what protects the reading experience, and breaking it defeats the whole design.
- Zero em-dash characters in any visible string.
- One accent colour. Do not introduce a second hue for XP, items, or stages.
- Sharp corners (radius 0) everywhere. Pills allowed only inside the creature HUD.
- Both light and dark tested.
- Any animation gated behind `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`.
- Server components by default. Add `'use client'` only for something genuinely interactive, and say why in your report.

## Edge cases that must render, not crash

| Case | Expected |
|---|---|
| `state.github === null` | Everything renders, ledger simply has no commit rows |
| Empty garden, `totalXp === 0` | Sporeling, empty bar, all items locked |
| Max stage | `nextStage === null`, `xpForNextStage === null`, progress bar full |
| `breakdown` empty | Ledger renders an empty state, does not crash |
